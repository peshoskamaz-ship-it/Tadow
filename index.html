Our story

A Lounge Born From Craft

Nestled within the lush grounds of Gradska Gradina park in the heart of Blagoevgrad, Tadow Cocktail Lounge opened its doors in 2023 with a single ambition: to make every drink a small journey. Our menu is built by bartenders who care deeply about flavour, balance, and surprise.

From the first espresso of the morning to a late-night cocktail under the terrace lights, Tadow blends an intimate indoor lounge — stone walls, velvet chairs, warm lamps — with a sun-warmed stone terrace that feels like the best-kept secret in town.

Not sure what to order? Tell us your mood and our bartenders will build something just for you.
