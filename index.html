Welcome

A lounge unlike any other

Nestled within the lush grounds of Gradska Gradina park in the heart of Blagoevgrad, Tadow Cocktail Lounge is where the city comes alive after sundown. Our space blends warmth, creativity, and an unmistakable sense of occasion.

Our creative cocktail menu spans continents — from botanical garden sippers to bold espresso-driven nightcaps — each drink crafted with intention and served with pride.

Can't find what you're after? Our skilled mixologists are delighted to craft a completely custom drink just for you. All you need to do is ask.
